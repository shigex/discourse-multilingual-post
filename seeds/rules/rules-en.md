# Bayview BBS Community Rules

## Introduction

Welcome to the Bayview BBS. This is an **unofficial online community forum** for residents of Bayview, where everyone can chat comfortably in their own language. It's not an official channel of any company, but rather a personal project created and managed by a resident named Shige. As a fellow resident, I volunteer to cover the server costs and moderate the forum, so please understand and support the project accordingly.

The purpose of this forum is simple.

- Share any issues or concerns you have regarding the shared facilities (kitchen, gym, and laundry).
- Exchange information about items being given away, sold, or that people are looking for.
- Share tips on great local restaurants, administrative procedures, and how to properly handle your trash.
- Plan and organize events and study groups.
- Through everyday conversations, you gradually get to know your neighbor's face and character.

LINE's open chat features and community forums do not offer the experience of everyone being able to read and write in their native language. Given the environment in Bayview, where approximately 80% of residents are foreign nationals, posts are automatically translated and displayed in each individual's language. When written in Japanese, the message will reach residents who speak Korean, English, German, Spanish, French, and Chinese. The same applies in reverse.

## Posting Guidelines

1. Please write with respect. Any discriminatory remarks regarding culture, religion, nationality, gender, sexual orientation, age, or occupation are prohibited. Even if intended as a joke, you cannot know how your words will be received.
2. Let's write constructively. When you clearly describe what you're struggling with, what you need help with, or what you'd like to share, you're more likely to receive positive responses from residents.
3. It's perfectly fine – and even preferable – to write in your native language. AI can then translate it for you. It's better to create natural, authentic posts by writing in your own language rather than trying to write perfect Japanese or English and becoming hesitant.
4. Please be mindful of sharing personally identifiable information. Do not post other people's full names, room numbers, or photos without their permission. When sharing your own information, please be aware of your audience and privacy settings.
5. Please select a category before posting. Transfer information goes to **Marketplace**, equipment issues to **Laundry / Gym / Facilities**, neighborhood questions to **Help / Tips**, and casual conversation to **Lounge**.

## Prohibited items

- Discrimination, harassment, and personal attacks.
- Soliciting for commercial purposes, recruitment, multi-level marketing, and the inclusion of affiliate links.
- Dating and sexually explicit content.
- Inciting or inducing illegal activity.
- Information that infringes on the privacy of other residents, such as full names, room numbers, and photographs.
- Excessive repeated posts, copied content, and spam.

You are welcome to share your opinions, criticisms, or suggestions for improvement regarding the management company (Bayview Corporation). As residents, your honest feedback is valuable to share amongst yourselves. However, please refrain from directly directing criticism of the management company through this forum. This is an informal space and not an official channel.

## Moderation Guidelines

- This forum is privately run by Shige. There are no professional moderators.
- If you find a post that you suspect may violate our guidelines, please let us know by using the **report button**. We welcome reports in any language, including those not in Japanese.
- After reviewing the report, we will take appropriate action, such as editing, making the post private, deleting it, or temporarily suspending the user. The final decision rests with Shige.
- If you are not satisfied with the decision, please contact us through the **Site Feedback** category, or directly via DM/LINE.

## Privacy

- You don't need to disclose your full name or room number. You can adjust your profile settings to display only the floor or to keep that information private.
- You are free to choose whether or not to use your own photos in your profile.
- Please do not post photos of other residents or children without their consent. Photos of common areas within Bayview are acceptable as long as they do not include any identifiable individuals.
- If you would like your past posts to be deleted when you leave, please contact Shige. We will generally accommodate such requests.

## About Translation

- Posts are automatically translated using a local LLM running on a Mac Mini. Because we do not use cloud translation services, both the original text and its translation remain on-site and are not sent to servers outside of Bayview.
- Machine translation is not perfect. In particular, it often misinterprets nuances such as sarcasm, puns, technical jargon, and regionally specific expressions.
- Clicking on the post will allow you to switch between the original text and the translation at any time. If you feel the translation is inaccurate, please refer to the original text.
- If your native language and the original language of the post are the same, the original text will be displayed as is.

## Contact information

- For questions posted on the forum, please use the **Site Feedback** category.
- For individual consultations, please use direct messages (DMs) within Discourse, or contact Shige directly through LINE. We will share the LINE ID with those who are interested.
- Operational issues and urgent communications are usually handled more quickly through LINE.

I hope this forum will add a little richness to your days in Bayview. I look forward to connecting with you.

— Shige
